# Fit evaluation — which opportunities fit Chris

Written Saturday 2026-10-10 after scanning the sources; saved 2026-10-11. This is the ranking that was previously only in
the chat. It rests on the profile below and on what each page said; items marked "unverified" came from general knowledge.

## Profile used for the scoring
- Rehm KI Consulting: freelance (§18), solo, started 1 Oct 2026 under a Gründungszuschuss, near Augsburg, AI process automation
  for the Mittelstand.
- Up to 25 billable days a month if the work exists (the IHK business plan assumed about 11).
- Background: Intel hardware design (VHDL, `e`), about six years of web development, a stack of early products (Notes World,
  Flashkarte and others).
- Defense work acceptable; art and USA excluded; Germany and Europe in scope.
- Values (from his charter): honesty, people over profit, craft, long-term thinking.

## Possible fits, ranked
1. **Forschungszulage** (rolling). R&D tax credit, 25% or 35% for SMEs of eligible costs; from 2026 own hours at €100/h
   (IHK and advisory-firm pages). Needs a defined R&D project with real technical uncertainty. Ask the Steuerberater whether a
   §18 freelancer qualifies and how it interacts with the Gründungszuschuss.
2. **Digitalbonus Bayern** (rolling, monthly quotas, to 31 Dec 2027). Clients apply themselves; a sales argument, not money to
   Chris. AI and robotics are new priorities.
3. **BayStartUP competitions 2027** (pre-registration open; €85,000 prize pool). No registered company needed; Bavarian
   residence is. The business plan already exists.
4. **Prototype Fund Jahrgang 03** (30 Nov 15:00 CET). Open-source software with social benefit; idea to prototype is fine.
   Fits only if he open-sources something.
5. **SPRIND AI-Native Chip Design** (30 Nov 11:00 CET, up to €9.6M). The biggest prize; needs a team and some validation.
6. **BayVFP Digitalisierung** (call window unknown). Bavarian company plus Bavarian university, up to 50% for industrial research.
   The route for the Augsburg professors, not EXIST.
7. **GovTecHH** (rolling). Idea-level pitch by email to startups@sk.hamburg.de.
8. **BMW Startup Garage** (rolling). Wants prototype maturity; a stretch for now.
9. **BAFA consulting subsidy** (guidelines to 31 Dec 2026). Clients get 50% of a €3,500 base, but the consultant must be
   BAFA-approved. Approval requirements unknown. A minor sales lever.
10. **NLnet** (3 Nov 12:00 CET). Its page says it is "not interested in AI-generated projects or proposals". Poor match.

## Later, not now
- **CIHBw pitch** (rolling) and **NATO DIANA** (annual call June 2027, dynamic challenges any time): defense work is acceptable,
  so these move up once he has a concrete product to pitch. CIHBw needs only an idea-level problem-and-solution deck.
- **EIC Accelerator**: needs TRL 6–8, so after a product exists.
- **Eurostars**: next cut-off probably early 2027 (unverified); needs a foreign partner and an R&D project.
- **Network sites with no open call found:** Bosch Startup Harbour, de:hub, UnternehmerTUM, DB mindbox (the Flämingo call page
  returned 404), appliedAI Institute, GovTech Deutschland.

## Not a fit
Sovereign Tech Agency (critical open-source maintainers), Cyberagentur (cyber research, no open call found), BMFTR (only Q1 2026
deadlines found), EXIST (the 5-year rule and the university as applicant), the Bavarian Justice Ministry IT-security
consulting tender (wrong specialty), and the practical-ai-act.eu site (a compliance resource, not a funder).

## Which need a product idea
- **Idea or pitch only:** GovTecHH, CIHBw, Prototype Fund ("from the idea to the software prototype"), NLnet, BayVFP.
- **Working prototype or proof:** BMW Startup Garage (prototype maturity), SPRIND chip challenge (asks for current status and
  validation), NATO DIANA (mature solutions), EIC (TRL 6–8), Eurostars (defined R&D project with a foreign partner), Bosch and
  DB mindbox (entry requirements unconfirmed).
- **No product needed:** Forschungszulage (needs an R&D project), BayStartUP (business plan), Digitalbonus and the BAFA subsidy
  (clients' projects), public tenders (services).

## Honest read
- Most opportunities found are startup-ecosystem programmes, not SBIR-style paid contracts. The realistic money levers for a
  solo consultant are the tax credit and the client subsidies, the competition and open-source funds, and SPRIND as the one big
  prize. The corporate programmes mostly want a product with proof.
- Several grant details came from secondary sources or search snippets and must be confirmed on official pages before relying
  on them.

## What was not scanned
ZIM (bot protection), Projektträger Jülich and DLR, Förderdatenbank, the tender portals (TED, service.bund.de, DTVP,
evergabe-online, Vergabe Bayern), the EU Funding Portal, and EDA/EUDIS.
