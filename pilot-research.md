# Pilot firm research — all six shortlisted firms

Sunday 2026-10-11. Web search only (no calls, no emails, no contact with any firm). Each fact has its source; "not found"
means the search did not return it, not that it does not exist. Distances are my estimates, not measured.

## Corrections to `pilot-shortlist.md`
1. **Aventus is not an insurer.** The search shows an insurance *broker* group, "Aventus Augsburg AG", a consortium of mehr-finanz24, Secura Gumpp, Peik Vogl, Dirr & Kollegen and Kindl und Schönberger, at Daimlerstraße 19, 86368 Gersthofen. The shortlist's "insurance application: 22 days" example does not apply directly.
2. **Firmengruppe APPL is in Wemding** (Donau-Ries district), not Augsburg.
3. **Bäckerei Luber could not be found at all** with two searches. Either the name or place in the register is slightly different, or the firm has almost no web presence.
4. **Rupp's real name is Michael Rupp Bauunternehmung GmbH, Weißenhorn.** Much smaller than I assumed.

## Ranking after research
1. **Rupp (Weißenhorn)** — owners decide alone, right size, curious about new technology, document-heavy business.
2. **Riegele (Augsburg)** — close, real processes, family decision.
3. **Aventus (Gersthofen)** — if it turns out to be a merged group with 20 to 100 people.
4. **CONLATA** — only if the merger is real and they have time.
5. APPL, 6. Luber — park.

## What I did not do
- No firm was contacted. Any approach should be a warm introduction or a reply to something they published (unsolicited advertising to businesses is restricted under the German UWG). Ask the IHK Schwaben if unsure.
- No staff names beyond what the sources print publicly (owners and managing directors) were collected.
- I did not look for owners' private details, and did not check works councils beyond a single search.
