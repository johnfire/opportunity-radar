## Details

### 1. Kinderwarnwesten aus ausgedienter Arbeitskleidung — 11 Oct — KOINNO — skip
The Stadtreinigung Hamburg wants partners to turn worn-out work clothing into children's warning vests, about 2,000 a year.
The process covers taking back cleaned clothing, sorting, removing fasteners, designing the vest, and making and delivering it.
It is a market sounding to find partners, including social enterprises. No payment is stated. It is physical manufacturing,
so AI adds little.
Link: https://app.koinnovationsplatz.de/challenges/6a9189b0f321e1a16eef0610

### 2. Die nächste Generation der Beihilfe-App — 12 Oct — KOINNO — draft written
The Landesamt für Besoldung und Versorgung Baden-Württemberg is replacing its "Beihilfe BW" app, with which about 327,000
people photograph medical receipts and submit them for reimbursement (about 2.4 million applications a year). It wants
market input before an EU-wide tender, expected in 2027, with a 5-year contract. The notice asks for a market-proven standard
product already in use, not custom development, and says taking part has no effect on the tender. No payment is offered for the
response. Our draft says plainly that we have no existing product and offers an on-premise AI document-understanding approach
plus tender advice.
Link: https://app.koinnovationsplatz.de/challenges/6a8c7479f321e1a16eee0041
Files: `/home/chris/Projects/koinno-beihilfe/` (draft `entwurf-einreichung.md`, `REVIEW-NOTES.md`, the two attachments).

### 3. Recoding Medicine — 16 Oct 18:00 CET — SPRIND — skip
A SPRIND challenge with three stages (€480k, €455k, €550k; 12, 12 and 6 months). Each team must bring at least one unique,
legally usable health dataset of high clinical or societal relevance and make a documented public contribution. I did not read
the full goal description. Without your own health data it is not applicable.
Link: https://www.sprind.org/en/actions/challenges/recoding-medicine

### 4. Next Frontier Robotics — 22 Oct 18:00 CEST — SPRIND — low
SPRIND wants full-stack robot platforms for complex physical tasks (logistics, care support, construction) or novel hardware
components such as grippers, sensors and joints. Funding is up to €7.2M per team across four stages over 36 months, as
fixed-price contracts based on the team's own cost estimate. Teams should incorporate a company, ideally before the start, and
show intent to build a self-sustaining business. Two tracks need separate applications.
Link: https://www.sprind.org/en/actions/challenges/next-frontier-robotics

### 5. Nachhaltiges Büromaterial — 25 Oct — KOINNO — skip
The Bezirksregierung Münster is preparing NRW's state-wide office-supplies framework contract for 2027 and asks suppliers about
sustainability certification, reusable shipping systems and last-mile transport. The real pain is comparable proof of
sustainability across thousands of articles, which an AI evidence-checking tool could address. But the questions are written for
suppliers, so as listed it is not ours. No payment is stated.
Link: https://app.koinnovationsplatz.de/challenges/6ab50896f321e1a16ef58136

### 6. Stellung von Mobilen Sanitäranlagen — 28 Oct — KOINNO — skip
The Bundeswehr-Dienstleistungszentrum Rostock is preparing a framework agreement for mobile toilets in four regional lots in
Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, with the tender planned for Q4 2026 and a 4–6 year term. It asks rental firms about the specification,
pricing of transport and setup, lead times and the lot structure. Its stated difficulty is estimating demand, which AI
forecasting could help with, but the call is for suppliers. No payment is stated.
Link: https://app.koinnovationsplatz.de/challenges/6abd55f6f321e1a16ef744a0

### 7. Moderne IT-Komponenten und mobile Endgeräte — 3 Nov — KOINNO — skip
The city of Tübingen is preparing an EU tender for IT peripherals and Apple mobile devices under a 4-year framework contract. It
asks about 3–5 working-day delivery, a minimum order of €30 or less, price stability for a year, repair and take-back, and
digital ordering and invoicing. The buyer estimates about €80,000 a year, which is purchasing volume, not money for respondents.
It is hardware reselling.
Link: https://app.koinnovationsplatz.de/challenges/6ab24230f321e1a16ef4f8e3

### 8. NLnet open-source grants — 3 Nov 12:00 CET — nlnet.nl — poor fit
NLnet funds open-source software, hardware and content with EU money; a first grant is €5k–50k and individuals can apply.
The open funds listed were Restack and CodeSupply plus a general call. Its page says it is "not interested in AI-generated
projects or proposals", which clashes with an AI-driven workflow. Applicants must come from a Horizon Europe-eligible country.
Link: https://nlnet.nl/propose/

### 9. Measuring with Entanglement — 9 Nov 12:00 CET — SPRIND — skip
SPRIND wants quantum sensors that beat the standard quantum limit using entanglement, in real-world conditions. Funding is up to
€545k, €1.05M and €1.68M over three stages, and the number of teams drops from up to 10 to 8 to 4. Teams must be incorporated by
Stage 1 or credibly by Stage 2, and selection sessions in Leipzig are mandatory. Quantum hardware is far from your profile.
Link: https://www.sprind.org/en/actions/challenges/measuring-with-entanglement

### 10. Engineering Microbial Consortia — 16 Nov 11:00 CET — SPRIND — skip
SPRIND wants circular biomanufacturing: fermentation with microbial communities, steered by AI/ML in design-build-test-learn
cycles, covering feedstock preparation through product recovery. Funding is up to €1.2M, €1.3M and €1.8M (total €4.3M) over three
stages, plus shared services outside the cap. It needs a wet lab and biology expertise. The AI-control part is relevant, but it
is only one piece.
Link: https://www.sprind.org/en/actions/challenges/engineering-microbial-consortia

### 11. Flämingo Accelerator Cohort #3 — 22 Nov — DB mindbox — needs reading

Flämingo Startup Hub accelerator as a free six-month programme for early-phase startups in mobility and logistics and in ICT and
creative industries, but that was for an earlier batch and I could not confirm the cohort #3 terms. Payment and eligibility are
unknown. Read the call page before deciding.
Link: https://www.dbmindbox.com/mindbox

### 12. AI-Native Chip Design — 30 Nov 11:00 CET — SPRIND with NADI — planning
SPRIND and the Dutch agency NADI want AI systems that autonomously run architecture search, RTL generation, verification and
design trade-offs, cutting chip development from years to weeks. Funding is up to €2.6M for 8 months, then up to €7.0M for 12
months (total €9.6M per team), as fixed-price contracts, with follow-on funding in the tens of millions targeted for tape-out.
German teams are eligible and a company need not exist yet. The web form is about 15,000 characters plus CVs, cost overview,
project plan and a sanctions declaration; feedback comes by 10 Dec. IP and equity terms are in the Participation Agreement,
which has not been read yet.
Link: https://www.sprind.org/en/actions/challenges/ai-native-chip-design
Form: https://www.sprind.org/en/actions/challenges/submissions/ai-native-chip-design

### 13. Prototype Fund, Jahrgang 03 — 30 Nov 15:00 CET — prototypefund.de
Funds open-source software with social benefit from individuals and small teams in Germany, from idea to prototype. The three
lines are Innovation (data security, software infrastructure), Resilienz (alternatives to Big Tech) and Up and Coming
(newcomers). Read on the official page 2026-10-10; the amount was not shown (a 2024 article gave up to €158k over ten months).
It fits only if you open-source something. Same day as the SPRIND chip deadline.
Link: https://www.prototypefund.de/

### 14. Cyber Innovation Hub der Bundeswehr — rolling — CIHBw
The Bundeswehr's digital innovation unit takes short problem-and-solution pitch decks at any time and contacts startups if the
idea matches current or upcoming projects. The aim is a prototype within 12–24 months; the hub only recommends solutions and
does not procure for the Bundeswehr itself. I could not retrieve a list of current topics or any amounts. Defense work is
acceptable to Chris.
Link: https://www.cyberinnovationhub.de/en/innovation/your-pitch-for-the-cihbw

### 15. EIC Accelerator — rolling, evaluation batch 4 Nov 17:00 Brussels
EU grant under €2.5M plus equity of €1–10M for single startups or SMEs with TRL 6–8 technology. Short proposals can be
submitted any time and are batched for evaluation. Individuals intending to launch an SME are named as eligible. Not for now;
it fits after a product exists.
Link: https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en

### 16. Forschungszulage — rolling
German R&D tax credit: 25%, or 35% for SMEs, of eligible R&D costs. From 2026 a sole proprietor's own hours count at €100/h and a
flat 20% covers overhead (per IHK and advisory-firm pages, not an official one). It rewards a defined R&D project with technical
uncertainty, not a product. Open points: whether a §18 freelancer qualifies and how it interacts with the Gründungszuschuss;
ask the Steuerberater. A certificate from the BSFZ comes first.

### 17. Digitalbonus Bayern — rolling, monthly quotas
Bavarian grant for small companies' digitalisation, relaunched to 31 Dec 2027, with AI and robotics as new priorities. Amounts
were not on the page (your note of 7 Sept says up to €30k). Clients must apply themselves; third-party filing is prohibited.
A sales argument for Rehm KI Consulting, not money to you. Check eligibility before applying.
Link: https://www.digitalbonus.bayern/

### 18. BayStartUP competitions, 2027 season — pre-registration open
Four regional business-plan competitions (Munich, Northern Bavaria, Ideenreich, Swabia) with expert feedback, an investor
network and a total prize pool of €85,000. Needs a company seat or a founder's residence in Bavaria; a registered company is
not required. Exact deadlines are not on the homepage. A business plan (yours is already written) is enough.
Link: https://www.baystartup.de/businessplan-wettbewerbe/

### 19. GovTecHH (Hamburg) — rolling
Hamburg's venture-client unit matches startups with the city administration's modernisation needs and runs pilot projects.
Payment terms are not stated. You pitch an idea for cooperation by email to startups@sk.hamburg.de. Public-sector process
automation is a fit with your theme.
Link: https://startupcity.hamburg/de/trends-themen/govtechh

### 20. BMW Startup Garage — rolling
BMW's venture-client unit: 4-month pilots, no equity, and it says over 50% of alumni got paid follow-on projects. Assessment
takes 4–8 weeks and weighs use-case relevance, prototype maturity, team and funding. Application form exists. A stretch until
you have a product to show.
Link: https://www.bmwstartupgarage.com/

### 21. BayVFP Digitalisierung — call window unknown
Bavaria's research-partnership programme: companies and Bavarian universities do joint pre-competitive R&D. Funding is up to
50% for industrial research and 25% for experimental development, with a surcharge of up to 15% for SMEs. It runs to 31 Dec
2029; applications go through the ELAN online system during call windows. Unknown: the next window, and whether a firm with no
employees can claim its own labor. Contact: Bayern Innovativ. This is the natural route for the Augsburg professor contacts.

### 22. NATO DIANA — next annual call June 2027
The 2027 challenges closed on 3 July 2026. DIANA also launches "dynamic challenges" at any time. Selected innovators get
€100,000 contractual funding, and some can get up to €300,000 more. It seeks mature dual-use solutions from companies in NATO
member states; start-ups and SMEs are welcome. Defense work is acceptable to Chris.
Link: https://www.diana.nato.int/challenges.html
