Read from the live pages on 2026-10-20 (challenge page, the public application form, and its FAQ). Nothing was submitted or filled in.

## Dates
- Deadline: **Thu 22 Oct 2026, 18:00 CEST**. Everything must be in English.
- Feedback within 14 days of the deadline. In-person pitches before the jury: 11–12 Nov. First invoice and start: 13 Nov.

## Money and shape
- 36 months, four stages, fixed-price contracts on the team's own cost estimate, up to **EUR 7.2 M per team**.
- Track 1 (Integrated Platforms): up to 1.0 M / 1.0 M / 2.2 M / 3.0 M per stage. Up to six teams start.
- Track 2 (Hardware Components, e.g. grippers, sensors, joints with a radical performance leap): up to 2.4 M / 2.2 M / 1.6 M / 1.0 M. Up to nine teams start.
- Pre-commercial procurement (PCP): SPRIND gets certain rights to the IP (see the Legal FAQ PDF and the Participation Agreement).
- Teams should aim to incorporate a company, ideally before the start. Individuals and duos may apply. EU, EFTA or UK location; a company must be headquartered there with at least 50 % of its equity there. No parallel public funding of the same work.

## Eligibility points that matter
- **Track 1 needs real hardware.** The FAQ says you must bring your own hardware system or an existing product of industrial-grade maturity, able to run and demonstrate core capabilities in the lab. "Theoretical concepts or early-stage ideas without a working prototype are not eligible." A pure AI or software team cannot apply to Track 1; the work must run on physical robots, and Track 1 is not for new AI architectures but for integrating existing models.
- Excluded use cases for Track 1: drones and other aerial systems, medical procedures on humans, underwater and floating systems. Focus is logistics, care support, construction, maintenance.
- Track 2 is for novel hardware components with a radical improvement over the state of the art.
- Applying to both tracks needs two separate forms; SPRIND warns that it checks whether the team can really do both.

## The form (all fields; character limits in brackets)
1. Personal details, institution (choose "No institution involved" for an individual), legal form (the list has "Individual Enterprise"), address, how you heard of SPRIND, why you apply.
2. Project title [50]; short description [800]; track selection.
3. Technological breakthrough: which "new frontier", what becomes possible that is not possible today [1000].
4. Detailed description and architecture of the end-to-end system [1000].
5. The key technical assumption that must hold, and the experiment that would prove it [800].
6. Technology readiness level (TRL 2 to 7+) and which evidence you can confirm and upload (lab tests, simulation, benchmark, publication, patent, customer pilot, production hardware, field test, independent evaluation).
7. Links to working artifacts: prototypes, code, models, demos [1000].
8. A specific first customer, their problem, why they buy from you, the added value [1000].
9. Biggest open risks [1000].
10. Legal entity status (existing, in formation, not yet formed) with roadmap to founding [1000].
11. Capital raised so far, by source: own funds, grants, angels, VC, strategic partners (amount and year).
12. The experiment or benchmark that would convince the jury at the end: what, conditions, expected measurable result, comparison with the state of the art [2000].
13. Work plan summary [3000]: whole challenge, Stage 1 work packages with partners and subcontractors, measurable KPIs.
14. Team: why you, relevant experience, missing expertise and how you fill it [2000].
15. Team members, one row each: name, role, percent FTE, track-record links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Scholar) [800 per row].
16. Declarations (tick yes/no): no grounds for exclusion under §123 and §124 GWB; no relevant infringements (minimum wage, posted workers). Data-protection consent. A simple "how many days in a week" spam check.

## Mandatory uploads (PDF)
1. **CVs of the key people**, at most 2 pages each.
2. **Detailed cost breakdown for Stage 1**: personnel, equipment and materials, external services and subcontracting, travel, overhead.
3. **Safety concept, 1 page**: safe operating limits, emergency stop procedures, risk mitigation, validation approach.
4. **Declaration on Russia sanctions** (SPRIND provides the document, German and English; must be signed).
5. **Detailed work plan** with work packages and milestones for Stage 1.
